OnDue Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

OnDue is a local-first app that helps you identify and manage obligations from your email. This policy explains what data OnDue accesses, how it is used, and your controls.

1) Data We Access

When you connect Gmail, OnDue requests read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly).

  • Email metadata (sender, subject, date, labels, thread ID)
  • Email content snippets and body text needed for extraction and classification

OnDue does not send emails, delete emails in Gmail, or modify your Gmail account.

2) How Data Is Used

  • To detect potential obligations and due-related items
  • To generate digest and coverage views
  • To improve in-app relevance based on your in-app actions

3) Where Data Is Stored

Data is stored locally on your device (SQLite database). OnDue does not upload your email content to OnDue-managed servers.

4) Retention

OnDue prunes older local messages according to your sync settings and selected range. You can also clear local cache or delete all account data from within the app.

5) Your Controls

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time
  • Reset local cache
  • Delete all account data stored by OnDue on this device
  • Control background sync and long-scan settings

6) Third-Party Services

OnDue uses Google Sign-In and Gmail APIs to access your mailbox with your permission. Your use is also subject to Google’s terms and privacy policies.

7) Children's Privacy

OnDue is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8) Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision.
